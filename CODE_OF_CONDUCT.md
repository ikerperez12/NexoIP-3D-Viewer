# Code of conduct

We want NexoIP 3D Viewer to be a respectful, useful, and harassment-free project for everyone.

Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, respect different experience levels, and keep technical discussions relevant. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or other conduct that makes participation unsafe is not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ikerperez12). Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
